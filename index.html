I'm Yifei Wu, an undergraduate student at Central South University (CSU), majoring in Bioinformatics. My current interests lie in T-cell immunity and the deep learning methods that support it — I have built a solid foundation in both fields, from sequence-level immune receptor data to the models that learn from it.

I am currently working on representation learning and generative modeling of immune repertoires (TRB and BCR), with applications to disease classification.
